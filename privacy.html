Harbor · Local-first

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026 · Full detail also in the repository docs/PRIVACY.md.

Harbor is a calm life-management app. In version 1 it is local-first: your plans live on your device. No Harbor account is required. No ads. We do not sell personal data.

Data on your device

Tasks, streaks, bills, groceries, subscriptions, household labels, calendar events you create or import, calendar feed URLs you paste, themes, and optional profile fields are stored in on-device storage (for example browser local storage or app storage). Clearing site data, uninstalling, or losing a device without a backup can erase this data.

What may leave your device

  • Weather: if you allow location, approximate location may be sent to a forecast provider (such as Open-Meteo) to show a forecast.
  • Calendar URL: Harbor fetches feeds directly from the calendar host by default. A network helper (third-party proxy) is off unless you enable it in Settings; if enabled, that helper may see the full feed URL (including secret tokens). Prefer Import file (.ics) for maximum privacy.
  • Feedback: messages you send may go through a feedback delivery service. Do not include secrets.
  • Hosting / updates: loading the web app or store updates may create ordinary server or CDN logs under those providers’ policies.

Sharing with others

Profile or data export creates a file on your device. You choose how to share it. v1 does not provide live cloud couple-sync.

Children

Harbor is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If we add accounts, cloud sync, or change calendar networking defaults, we will update this policy before those features ship.

Contact

Use in-app Feedback, or the support contact on the App Store / Play listing when published.
